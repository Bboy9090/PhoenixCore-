## ⚖️ General Palette Usage Guidelines

1.  **Workstation Focus**: Keep window backgrounds dark navy or obsidian to guarantee comfort during long daily-use sessions, allowing vibrant active states to pop.
2.  **Sovereign Trim Rules**: Gold should be used exclusively as thin, crisp borders, title styling, and logo accents. Avoid solid gold backgrounds for controls.
3.  **Controlled Red Accents**: Never overuse red in default configurations. Red is reserved mainly for:
    *   Arcwyre and Native specific edition theme states.
    *   System warning dialogs and error logs.
    *   Window Close buttons.
    *   Thundergod's specific visual gem/scarf highlights.
4.  **Electric Lighting**: Glow effects must stay elegant. Restrict blue and cyan glowing elements to active controls, hovered buttons, and selected text inputs.
