# ARCWYRE Edition Branding

The ARCWYRE Edition is the professional backbone of the Bobby’s Worldwide OS platform. It focuses on clean, high-contrast, cyber-security oriented visuals.

## Visual Pillars
- **Circuit Logic**: Clean lines and grid-based layouts.
- **Metallic Precision**: Use of slate and silver tones for a professional feel.
- **Data Clarity**: High-legibility typography and information-dense dashboards.
