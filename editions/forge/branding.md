# Forge Edition Branding

The Forge Edition is the industrial workhorse of Bobby’s Worldwide OS. It is designed for technicians who require absolute reliability and clear status indicators in the field.

## Visual Pillars
- **Hazard Awareness**: Bold orange accents for critical information.
- **Structural Integrity**: Dark, solid backgrounds that minimize glare.
- **Industrial Utility**: Practical, no-nonsense interface elements.
