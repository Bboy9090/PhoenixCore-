# PhoenixCore Brand System V2

PhoenixCore is the connected diagnosis, repair-planning, media-building, and deployment ecosystem of Bobby's Workshop.

## Shared design DNA

All products share:

- Command Navy or Workshop Black structural fields
- Phoenix Blue and Electric Cyan connection lines
- steel-white highlights
- one controlled product accent
- modular circuit, scan, route, USB, device, and vault motifs

The applications must look like one coordinated platform while remaining distinguishable by silhouette and accent.

## Production rules

- SVG files are the canonical source masters.
- Do not bake proof-sheet labels, filenames, checkerboards, or UI screenshots into assets.
- Green is a validation state, not the permanent PhoenixCore Mobile brand color.
- Orange belongs to media creation and deployment, not ARCWYRE Live.
- Brown is prohibited as the primary Repair Planning Engine identity.
- Product icons must remain legible at 24 px and retain their identifying silhouette without text.
