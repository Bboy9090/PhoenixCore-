# Phoenix Welcome

Phoenix Welcome is the first-run application for Phoenix OS.

Future responsibilities:

- Confirm network, display, input, audio, Bluetooth, printer, and update readiness.
- Guide browser, Flatpak app store, creator, streaming, gaming, and productivity setup.
- Explain recovery superpowers without making Phoenix OS feel recovery-only.
- Offer safe entrypoints into Phoenix Control Center, BootForge, and Phoenix Key.
